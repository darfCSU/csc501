**System Selection: Frosmear Legacy Modernization**

For my Portfolio Project, I have selected a system modernization project for a fictitious technology company initially named Schumann Hudson Acquisition & Technology.  The project will transform Frosmear.com, a legacy PHP-based website focused primarily on online games, into a modern, cloud-oriented application platform called Darfcloud. The proposed system will use Docker containers and the Django web framework to support artificial intelligence concepts, general-purpose applications, and tools for evaluating investment opportunities. The project will focus on the management and planning required for a successful modernization, including requirements analysis, resource allocation, scheduling, risk management, data migration, security, stakeholder communication, and organizational change. The goal is to preserve relevant legacy functionality while establishing a maintainable, scalable foundation for future development.
